# ArunikaDungeon v0.5.0 — Private Dungeon & Universal Ticket
Development source preview for Paper 1.21.11 / Java 21. Continues the exact v0.4.0 source archive. Production gateway and rewards remain disabled. Do not remove CdrDungeonSpawner, ArunikaDungeonExchange, CdrWaystone or its Bedrock bridge.

## What's new
- Private party creation (solo included), 60-second invitations, accept/decline, readiness, kick, transfer, disband/leave, and ten-minute idle/queue expiry. Maximum 5 including leader; each floor may use a lower limit. Shared queue, Private party and active/reserved session use a common UUID lock.
- Per-floor FIFO pending parties; one centrally polled queue, no per-player scheduler. The global Private active-session limit (default 3) and configured non-overlapping arena pool are enforced by the existing session/battle engine. Performance guard pauses launches. Holding an eligible ticket is checked again before launch.
- One universal PDC ticket (`dungeon_ticket_serial` and `dungeon_ticket_generation`) pays for the entire party, all floors, no Vault/payment currency. Each issued item is individually serialized, normally nonstackable. Renamed ordinary paper and unknown serials cannot open an encounter. Ticket spends, commit, refunds and final settlement are durable per-ticket records under `data/tickets/` (atomic replacement on supported filesystem). Only one redemption of a generation; a refund advances generation, permanently invalidating old copies.
- Arena validation/reservation and ready checks precede payment. Hold occurs before teleport; commit and item consumption after BattleEngine.start, before another battle tick. No item is consumed for waiting/cancelled parties. A failed launch after hold is refunded. Recognized internal battle errors, missing tracked mob, staff stop and clean shutdown refund; defeat or time expiry consume the ticket. Unconfirmed committed sessions after hard crash require staff verification. No automatic refund for a committed ticket on restart.
- Claim is recorded until inventory delivery is acknowledged; full inventories/offline refunds remain claimable by payer. Duplicate physical copies after interrupted inventory/ledger acknowledgment cannot redeem more than once. Pending ticket IDs appear in logs or issuance/refund messages.
- Version 0.4 queue/cooldown and earlier migration/battle features retained. New read-only private status method on gateway API; real Waystone UI integration remains a later milestone.
